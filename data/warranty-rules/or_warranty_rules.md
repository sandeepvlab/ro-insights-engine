# Oregon Warranty Reimbursement Rules

- Statutory/reference labor rate: $160/hour for warranty repairs
- Parts markup allowance: dealer cost + approximately 10%, up to a documented average retail markup ceiling
- Submission window: claims must be filed within 30 days of repair order closure
- Required documentation for submission:
  - Signed and closed Repair Order (RO)
  - Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
  - Itemized labor operations with hours and rates
  - Itemized parts list with part numbers and pricing
  - Technician diagnostic findings and cause/correction narrative
- Eligible payment classifications: WARRANTY, EXTENDED_WARRANTY
- Not eligible: CUSTOMER_PAY, collision/cosmetic damage not tied to a covered component failure

This document is a synthetic reference for demo purposes only and does not represent actual Oregon state law or OEM policy.
